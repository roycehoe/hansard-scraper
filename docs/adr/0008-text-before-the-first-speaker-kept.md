# 0008. Text before the first speaker kept

Status: accepted, 15 October 2026. Supersedes [0005](0005-text-before-the-first-speaker-dropped.md). Stored rows are not rewritten yet.

Context. [0005](0005-text-before-the-first-speaker-dropped.md) kept the rule that drops every line before the first speaker is set (`is_in_preamble`). Its reason, from commit `156ca70` of 30 May 2026, was a pass metric that rose from 96.9% to 99.8% once no-speaker rows stopped being created, and no researcher need was recorded. Round 2 of the speech fidelity loop and the write #3 stale checker showed the cost. The checker compares each stale report's stored rows with what HEAD produces. 355 of the 477 strict-match reports were held only because HEAD drops preamble text the stored rows hold today: question stems ("The following Question stood in the name of ..."), notices of motion, resolutions, oath rolls, "Order read" lines and bill titles. With the preamble drop accepted, 462 of the 477 were explained and 15 held. In all, 359 reports hold such text. A rebuild of those reports under 0005 would delete text from the table that is there now. Reports 31083 and 42017, which 0005 and the register name, show the same loss: 31083 is a Budget speech with no speaker label, and 42017 is a notice of motion.

Decision. The user decided on 15 October 2026 to keep the text before the first speaker as speech rows with no speaker, and to supersede 0005. `_parse_speeches` no longer skips lines while no speaker is set (commit `e37853a`): each such paragraph becomes a row with `speaker` null, in source order and ahead of the first speech. The rows are kept only when a speaker label follows; a report with no label at all still falls through to the single-speaker path. Report header fields, bold, parenthesised or italic title lines, headings and time markers stay dropped. The report's own title line is still not a row, so older-system reports still start after the line that matches the title. Newer-system reports start at the top of the body, so their lines before the first label become rows. The raw HTML and `Report.markdown_content` are unchanged. The speaker index is not affected: a row with no speaker links to no member.

Each of these rows gets a kind by content ([0009](0009-speech-kind-column.md)). A line a rule recognises takes that rule's kind: an "Order read" line, "Order for Third Reading read" or an oath roll is PROCEDURAL, order-paper questions ("To ask the Minister ...") and motion text ("That this House ...") are DOCUMENT_TEXT, and a "Head NNNN -" fragment is HEADING, which the stopping rule grades Tier C. A line no rule recognises is UNCLASSIFIED. The row sits in the preamble by position. Position is not a kind.

An unlabelled opening speech is the one case where the row is speech. The mover's opening speech in report 24248, a debate on the President's Address, has its label merged into the heading. It is kept as 30 rows with no speaker and kind SPEECH, named in `data/unattributed_speech.json` ([0009](0009-speech-kind-column.md)), and 24248 joins the per-report Tier A list in the [register](../data-loss-register.md). The Budget speech in report 31083 is listed the same way: the parse already leaves its rows without a speaker, and the list gives them kind SPEECH. Both stay unattributed until a separate fix labels them.

Consequences. 9,241 rows are added in 5,729 reports, none of them with a speaker. The 13 October count of 5,101 no-speaker rows ahead of the first speaker (Parliaments 0 to 11) shows the scale the 30 May rule removed. Ordinals in an affected report shift by the number of rows inserted, so the write updates the ordinals of every later row. The shipper sends `id`, `ordinal` and `kind` alone for a row whose only changes are its ordinal and its new kind: 599,073 of 626,820 updates. No existing row id is deleted by this change. A bill title, a list of names or a table in a preamble becomes one row per paragraph.

The attribution figures move as follows (Parliaments 0 to 11), and all three must be quoted. The ADR 0001 rate does not move through this change: the new rows have no speaker string, so they leave its numerator and its denominator alone. The all-rows rate falls, because its denominator counts every row. In the rehearsal the preamble commit adds 5,433 rows to that denominator and moves 22 linked characters out of linked rows, so text share does not move through it. Across the whole of write #3, text dropped inside reports that have rows falls from 0.40% to 0.19%, and the share of body text in rows with no speaker rises from 1.21% to 1.63%. The preamble rows are one cause among several; the round log has the breakdown. The all-rows rate falls with no speech lost, as it did when [0007](0007-non-speech-lines-get-no-speaker.md) nulled speakers.

Alternatives. Keep dropping the lines, as in 0005: the pass metric and the all-rows rate stay higher, 359 stale reports stay held and every rebuild deletes stored text. Keep only motion text and unlabelled opening speeches: not built. Leave these rows without a kind: they would be NULL-speaker rows again, indistinguishable from speech with an unknown speaker. Add a row-type column: built, see [0009](0009-speech-kind-column.md).
