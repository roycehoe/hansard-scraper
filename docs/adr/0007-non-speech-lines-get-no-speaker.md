# 0007. Non-speech lines get no speaker

Status: accepted, 14 October 2026, extended 15 October 2026.

Context. An unbolded line after a member's turn inherited the member as speaker. Procedural lines ("Question put, and agreed to.", "Resolved,", "Adjourned accordingly at ..."), division lists, editorial notes and "rose" stage directions were stored as that member's speech. The strata audit counted about 19,000 rows matching a procedural anchor and logged the error in 7 of every 10 budget and motion reports. In Parliaments 0 to 11, about 9,600 linked rows held text no member spoke.

Decision. Commit `be4d7dd` of 14 October 2026 gives no speaker to three kinds of line. After a procedural line the parser resets the current speaker, except after "Question proposed" and "*The same amendment also stood in the name of", where the mover often carries on. A whole-line editorial note gets no speaker: vernacular pointers, Standing Order 22(3) notes, chair notes, supply headers, "[Laughter]", chamber entries and copies handed in. A "rose" stage direction gets no speaker, in the bold form "**Mr X** rose -", the plain form "Mr X rose -" and the forms "rose and then resumed his seat" and "rose - [Interruption]". The bold name is not kept: the row reads "rose -" with no speaker. A targeted UPDATE applied the same result to stored rows. Commit `b1de4f2` of 15 October 2026 adds recorded question decisions ("Question, 'That ...' put, and agreed to.") as procedural lines that keep the turn, and a "Clause" or "Schedule ordered to stand part" line that ends it.

Consequences. 12,245 stored rows lost their speaker and speaker_id, 11,135 of them linked. A re-parse of 41,697 reports moved 12,255 rows to no speaker and moved none to a different speaker. Four changed rows in the stale reports 26467 and 27699 could not be matched and were left alone. Hand reads of every line kind found real speech in 6 rows only: a Budget statement in report 22691, resumed after a suspension, was credited to Mr Speaker and is now unattributed. One heading fragment in report 13940 drops. The bold "rose" form covers 1,014 rows, 1,001 of them linked, each linked to the member with no speech in the row.

The stage direction keeps no name. Who rose is gone from the row. The raw HTML keeps it, and the records rank "keep the person's name on a rose stage direction" among the next fixes with a corpus count not measured. Eight more "rose ---" rows lost their speaker on 15 October 2026 when the html2text escape fix exposed them, and the user accepted that. The question-decision rule covers 664 rows, from the handoff line that lists it with three other fixes.

Figures moved as follows (Parliaments 0 to 11): ADR 0001 rate 97.61% to 97.74%, all-rows rate 90.42% to 88.88%, text share 96.81% to 96.58%. The rate that rose is the one the ADR 0001 denominator rewards when a speaker becomes null, so quote all three. `docs/report/non-speech-repair/snapshot_before.tsv` holds every target's speaker and speaker_id before the write, which is enough to undo it.

Alternatives. Leave the speaker on these lines, as before: the all-rows rate and text share stay higher and the speaker index credits members with text they did not speak. Keep the stage direction's speaker: it would credit the member with a row that holds no speech of theirs, and nulling the bold form costs about 0.15 points of the all-rows rate with no speech lost. Add a row-type or confidence column to mark these rows: out of scope in the peer-learnings brief, decision 1.
