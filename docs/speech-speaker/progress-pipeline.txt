## Iteration 1 — 2026-06-02

### OCR variant "Tha"/"Chia" for "Tai"/"Chai" in Lai Tai Chai

**Fix type:** Manual override entries in `_MANUAL_OVERRIDES`

**Candidates targeted:**
- `Lai Tha Chai` — volumes 32–51, total ~1,033 sitting-markdown occurrences, parl=3–6
- `Lai Tha Chia` — volume 50 only, 3 occurrences, parl=6

**Evidence:** `Lai Tai Chai` (PAP, Henderson) is in Speaker table at parliament_number=3,4,5,6.
Confirmed first elected 1972. Volume range 32–51 maps exactly to parl=3–6 in VOLUME_TO_PARLIAMENT.
No occurrences in volumes 12–23 (colonial era). Override is a correct attribution.

**Overrides added to `services/attendance.py`:**
