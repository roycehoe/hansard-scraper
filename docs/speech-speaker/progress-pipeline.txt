## Architecture Refactor — 2026-06-02

### Replace parliament-scoped OCR entries with _OCR_CORRECTIONS dict

**Change:** All OCR character-substitution overrides moved from parliament-scoped
`_MANUAL_OVERRIDES` entries to a new parliament-agnostic `_OCR_CORRECTIONS` dict.
Applied as a preprocessing step in `resolve_canonical_name` before the cascade.

**Why:** OCR errors are scanner artifacts independent of parliament. Scoping them to
specific parliament numbers encodes parliamentary history rather than the OCR error,
and required manually enumerating every parliament a person served in.

**Result:**
- ~64 parliament-scoped entries → ~52 entries in `_OCR_CORRECTIONS`
- `_MANUAL_OVERRIDES` now reserved for: surname disambiguation, inverted canonicals
- Architecture documented in `docs/speech-speaker/pipeline-fix-loop.md`
